Royal Chess - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Publisher: BR Astra Studio.

What Royal Chess collects

Royal Chess is an offline chess game. It has no accounts or login. It does not ask for, collect, store or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details. Your games are played on your device and are not sent to any server of ours.

No ads, no analytics

The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Third-party services

The Microsoft Store version loads the game from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. The game may also download a font file from Google Fonts. As with any website, these providers may automatically record technical information such as your IP address and browser type when a page or file is requested. We do not receive, access or use that information.

Children

Royal Chess does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions, please use the support contact shown on the Royal Chess page in the Microsoft Store.
