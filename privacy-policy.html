Royal Chess – Privacy Policy

Royal Chess is an offline chess game. It does not require an account or login.

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device. Your games are not sent to any server.

The game does not use advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.
